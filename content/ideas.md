This page outlines the ideas I am aiming to work on in the future. See what I'm doing [now](/now) and [about](/about) how I got here.

If any ideas standout as interesting to you, [contact me](/contact), and let's talk or collaborate :)

## Big

- **How can fiction be used to convey ideas?**

  In short, I recently came to understand the power of fiction as a tool for conveying ideas that must be lived, not just explained ([I wrote a blog post on this topic](/the-fiction-experiment)).

  As a result, I am writing short, fictional stories of my own that try to convey powerful ideas. [Read them here.](/short-stories)

- **How can satellite data help smallholder farmers?**

  This all started with a dive down a rabbit hole about how I can use the skills I have in web dev, data science, and cloud computing to contribute to meaningful problems. I even went as far as creating [my own masters degree](https://github.com/bealonso2/climate-ml-masters) of sorts.

  As it turns out, there is this field called "Earth Observation" which leverages often publicly available satellite data to create impactful products. This is the world I am trying to live in. In particular, I want to challenge myself to see if it is possible to provide meaningful data to smallholder farmers using even the most basic insights that can be gleaned from satellite data.

  [Link to the project on GitHub](https://github.com/bealonso2/openfarmmap)

## Small

- **Slow social media**

  The internet is an amazing technology. It has the power to connect humans from every corner of the Earth. How absurd is this?

  I want the internet to serve as a place I go to connect and exchange ideas with likeminded humans.

  Unfortunately, if we aren't careful, the internet can be a bottomless pit of entertainment ([see related](/2012-rosy-retrospection)) or an unhealthy competition for attention in the form of followers, views, and likes ([see related](/my-journey-discovering-who-i-am)).

  To address this, I'm beginning to dive into the indie web. My understanding of this new world is still rudimentary, but the premise seems to be: own your platform and connect on your own terms.
