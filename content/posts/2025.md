2025 was a turbulent year to say the least. I both married my soulmate and lived through a family health scare. Through these highs and lows, I feel I have matured a tremendous amount and have gained much clarity on what actually matters to me—in turn, lifting a big weight off of my shoulders.

2025 was also the year I started writing on [Bear](https://bearblog.dev), coincidentally discovering the [IndieWeb](https://indieweb.org) and getting to meet some wonderful people :). Over three years after beginning to publish writing online, I feel I have finally found my home in the wide world of personal websites.

For safe-keeping, here are some other things I am proud to have done in 2025:

- Completed my first bodyweight [Murph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_P._Murphy#Murph_workout) in ~1 hour
- [Realizing I already had what I wanted](https://balonso.com/realizing-you-already-have-what-you-want/) including:
  - A fulfilling, impactful career path
  - A distraction-free, no-algorithm music listening experience through CDs and records
- Published a few [short stories](https://balonso.com/short-stories/), including an allegory outlining what I think it means to live a fulfilling life
- Bought my first, second, and third pair of barefoot shoes
- Finally, [breaking my short-form content addition](https://balonso.com/its-not-dopamine-that-makes-short-form-content-digital-crack/)
- Cancelled ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot subscriptions in an effort to rely on my own intuition and skill again

---

As a result of the clarity I gained in 2025, my desires for 2026 are focused.

This year I feel motivated to come at my work as a craftsman; I don't feel the need to take on side-projects; I want to keep learning and sharing things (on this site) that interest me; and when the motivation presents itself, I am excited to use [the power of fiction](https://balonso.com/the-fiction-experiment/) and write more short stories.

Life feels fairly well balanced right now, so I'm really just striving for "more of the same" in 2026.

Happy new year!
